AI-Powered Employee Experience Platform

Unified internal workspace for productivity, communication, and automation.

Company
Kapture CX ↗
Role
UX Designer
Expertise
Platform UX
Year
2025

Kapture CX needed a unified internal workspace to replace a fragmented set of tools spread across HR, communications, task management, and automation workflows. The goal was to design a single platform that could serve multiple employee personas — from frontline support agents to managers and HR teams — without overwhelming any of them.

Three strategic principles shaped the design process from brief to delivery.

Multi-persona research
Conducted structured interviews and workflow shadowing across four distinct employee types: support agents, team leads, HR administrators, and senior managers. Each had different priorities, cadences, and pain points that the platform needed to accommodate simultaneously.
Information architecture
Mapped the full scope of tasks, workflows, and data the platform needed to handle. Ran card sorting and tree testing exercises to find a navigation structure that felt intuitive across all personas — surfacing the right things to the right people without surfacing everything to everyone.
Progressive disclosure
Reduced cognitive load by revealing advanced options only when needed. Default views were designed for the most frequent tasks, with deeper controls available one level in — not hidden, just appropriately deferred.

Four distinct product surfaces were designed and delivered as part of the platform.

Unified dashboard
A role-adaptive home surface that surfaces each user's most relevant tasks, pending approvals, announcements, and metrics at a glance. Layout and content shift based on role and recent activity — no manual configuration required.
AI automation panel
An embedded workflow builder that allows non-technical users to automate repetitive HR and operations tasks — scheduling, onboarding checklists, escalation routing — using natural language triggers and pre-built connectors.
HR workflow hub
A centralised space for leave requests, performance reviews, payroll queries, and document management. Designed to reduce email back-and-forth by making request status, approvals, and history visible to all parties involved.
Communication command centre
Team announcements, group channels, and direct messaging consolidated under one roof — with deep links between conversations and the tasks or documents they reference, so context is never lost.

The platform was designed to scale with the organisation, not just serve its current size.

Scalable component system
Built a shared design system with 60+ components and 8 layout patterns — covering all four surfaces and documented in Figma for handoff. Component variants support all role, state, and data-density combinations without bespoke design.
Role-based permissions
Access controls are surfaced at the design layer — not buried in admin settings. Managers can see what their team sees, and HR can preview employee-facing flows, reducing configuration errors during rollout.
Analytics and feedback
An integrated insights layer gives leadership visibility into platform adoption, task completion rates, and workflow bottlenecks — with enough context to act, not just observe.

Outcome

Designing for multiple personas in one platform is a problem of priorities, not features. The real design work was deciding what to surface, when, and to whom — and building enough flexibility into the system that the answer could change as the organisation grew.
