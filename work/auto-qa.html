AI-Powered Quality Assurance Platform

Centralized quality evaluation platform for support agents.

Company
Kapture CX ↗
Role
UX Designer
Expertise
UX/UI Design
Year
2025

Auto QA is Kapture CX's AI-powered platform for evaluating customer support agent conversations at scale. Before it existed, quality audits were manual, sporadic, and subjective — reviewers would sample a fraction of conversations, score them inconsistently, and deliver feedback too late to change behaviour. The platform was designed to make quality evaluation continuous, structured, and actionable.

Three overlapping research and design tracks ran in parallel throughout the project.

Workflow mapping
Shadowed QA analysts, team leads, and support managers to understand how quality evaluation actually worked — not how it was supposed to work. Uncovered that most feedback was delivered too late, too vaguely, and without enough context for agents to act on it.
Dual-persona design
The platform serves two distinct users with opposing needs: evaluators (who need structured tools to assess conversations quickly and fairly) and agents (who need feedback that is specific, timely, and tied to real examples). Both personas were designed for in parallel, not sequentially.
AI output readability
AI-generated evaluation scores are only useful if a human can understand why. Worked closely with the ML team to define how model outputs should be presented — score breakdowns, citation-linked evidence, confidence indicators — so evaluators could trust, verify, and override the AI when needed.

Six core features define the Auto QA platform experience.

Conversation scoring
AI-evaluated scores for every conversation, broken down by quality dimensions (tone, resolution, compliance, empathy). Each dimension links directly to the moment in the transcript that influenced the score — so evaluators can review evidence, not just conclusions.
AI evaluation criteria
A visual rubric builder that lets QA managers define what "good" looks like for their team — weighted scoring, custom criteria, pass/fail thresholds — without writing a single line of code. Criteria can be versioned and A/B tested across teams.
Agent performance timeline
Each agent has a longitudinal performance view showing score trends, recurring issues, and improvement areas over time. Designed to surface coaching opportunities rather than just compliance violations.
Team leaderboards
Configurable team and individual rankings that QA leads can share in team standups or 1:1 reviews. Designed to encourage healthy competition without creating anxiety — rankings are opt-in and framed around improvement, not surveillance.
Calibration workflows
A structured process for QA teams to align on scoring standards — reviewers score the same conversation independently, then compare results, discuss discrepancies, and lock in a calibrated benchmark. Reduces evaluator bias over time.
Dispute and appeal flow
Agents can flag scores they disagree with, attach context, and request re-review — all within the platform. Designed to give agents agency in the process and reduce the perception that AI evaluation is final and unappealable.

Four structural decisions made the platform viable at enterprise scale.

Bulk evaluation
QA analysts can queue hundreds of conversations for batch review, apply filters (date range, agent, channel, score range), and process evaluations with a single scoring action — dramatically reducing manual effort without removing human oversight.
CRM data integration
Conversation data, agent profiles, and team structures pull directly from existing CRM sources. No duplicate data entry, no manual imports — evaluations are always in context with the full customer record.
Customisable scoring
Different business units, geographies, and product lines can run entirely different quality frameworks within the same platform — with isolated rubrics, separate leaderboards, and independent calibration sessions.
Real-time alerts
Configurable threshold alerts notify team leads when an agent's score drops below a defined level, or when a specific quality dimension trends negatively — enabling intervention before patterns become problems.

Outcome

AI evaluation is only as trusted as its transparency. The hardest design challenge on Auto QA wasn't building the scoring UI — it was making the AI's reasoning legible enough that evaluators would act on it, and agents would believe it was fair. That required designing for doubt, not just clarity.
