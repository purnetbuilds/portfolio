        How I think · 03 / 04
        
Skills I use to design
systems that ship.

      

      
Six heuristics I keep returning to — from first sketch to the Tuesday-morning fire drill after launch.

    

    

      

        
01 — Foundations

        

          
        

        
Systems thinking

        
Architecture before screens. Think in components, states, and relationships — less rework later.

      

      

        
02 — Exploration

        

          
        

        
AI-assisted breadth

        
More scenarios, explored faster. AI as a co-pilot for breadth, not a shortcut for depth.

      

      

        
03 — Craft

        

          
        

        
Build-ready design

        
Implementation constraints baked in from the first sketch. No designs that can't be built.

      

      

        
04 — Partnership

        

          
        

        
Strategic collaboration

        
Balance user needs with business goals. Partnership with PM and Eng — not a handoff.

      

      

        
05 — Business

        

          
        

        
Business-aware design

        
Feasibility, metrics, long-term strategy shape every decision — not just the happy path.

      

      

        
06 — Cadence

        

          
        

        
Rapid iteration

        
Fast feedback loops. Short cycles. Small bets. Quick refinement over grand reveals.
